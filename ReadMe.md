### About

Full-stack engineer with a frontend-heavy foundation and growing backend depth. I build product interfaces that stay fast under real traffic and backend services where correctness matters — payments in particular.

- **Now** — Software Engineer at [Eticsoft](https://eticsoft.com/), building payment integrations and orchestration services
- **Before** — Primary frontend developer at Bilet & More, owning ~90% of a Vue/Nuxt codebase
- **Writing** — Technical articles in English and Turkish on [Medium](https://medium.com/@ertugrulyaman99) (render cycles, Virtual DOM, payment patterns)
- **Based in** — Antalya, Türkiye · open to remote collaboration
